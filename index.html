Mission briefing

Clear the annex.

The inspection drones have turned hostile. Locate and disable all fourteen in a single-player operation that stretches from the pump room to the roof.

Track
Scan for a drone. The floor plan draws a route through doors and up the stairs to its signal.
Engage
An amber flash signals incoming fire. Break line of sight or move clear of the energy orb.
Recover
Armor recharges between encounters. If your camera goes offline, redeploy with cleared drones still down.

Facility index

Explore the annex.

Two switchback stairwells connect four playable levels. Select a floor to see inside.

Equipment & optics

On camera.

Two distinct loadouts

Switch between the service pistol and compact carbine. Each has its own recoil, draw, and reload timing. Watch the hand, magazine, and weapon move independently.

Body-camera optics & mic

Steep wide-angle distortion, backlit glass grime, rolling-shutter shear, high-ISO shadow grain, auto-exposure ducking, and compressed chest-mic audio turn every heel-strike and gunshot into raw field footage. Toggle the tactical LED in dark rooms.

512 px to 4K textures

Choose from four resolutions in Graphics. Scanned concrete, roof bitumen, and worn metal render with pooled area lights, per-floor culling, and wet-floor reflections at 1K and above.

Camera archive
