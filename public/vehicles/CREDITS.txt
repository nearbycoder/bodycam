The optimized mesh references 13 external maps through the texture-quality control.
Native sizes include a 2048×2048 ambient-occlusion atlas, 1024×1024 tire sidewall
color and normal maps, a 1024×256 dashboard, and 512×128 tread detail. Other native
maps range from 32 to 512 px. Higher settings preserve each map's source resolution.
